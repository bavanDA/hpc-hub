Troubleshooting

What to do when a cluster won't connect, a terminal closes, Duo times out or the phone drops the connection.

Can't reach the VPN or the cluster on some networks#

The card says something like Can't reach Wulver from this network. Is the VPN on? or Can't get through to Yale from this network., but the same thing worked at home.

Some networks block VPN connections or SSH: guest Wi-Fi, eduroam on some campuses, hotels and conference networks. There is nothing to fix in the app. Try another network, or your phone's mobile data (a hotspot works for the laptop too), then press Try again.

A pasted script closed my terminal#

You pasted a script into a terminal, a command in it failed, and the session ended (This session has ended.).

The script most likely has set -e near the top. It tells the shell to quit as soon as a command fails. In a script, that stops the script. Pasted into a terminal, it stops the terminal's own shell, and the session ends with it.

Save the script to a file and run it with bash, in a new terminal:

nano setup.sh      # paste the script, save
bash setup.sh

It runs in its own shell, so when it stops, your terminal stays.

Duo timed out#

The card says Duo wasn't approved in time. Click Try again and approve the push on your phone.

  • Duo pushes expire after about a minute. While the app waits it shows a timer; after a while it offers Send a new push.
  • Check that the Duo app on your phone can receive pushes: open it once, and make sure its notifications are on.
  • The HPC Hub phone app can tell you when Duo is waiting (Settings, Notifications).
  • Then press Try again on the card.

The phone app drops the connection in the background#

Android closes connections of apps in the background to save battery. In the phone app, open Settings, On the phone, and turn on both:

  • Stay connected in the background
  • Run without battery limits

Some phones have extra battery savers of their own. If it still drops, look for HPC Hub in the phone's battery settings and allow it to run in the background. Your terminal sessions keep running on the cluster either way; they reattach when the phone connects again.

Waiting a long time for a node#

Waiting for a free node usually takes under a minute, longer when the cluster is busy. A smaller node, a shorter time or another queue often starts sooner; Usage, Queues shows how busy each one is. Also check for the maintenance warning.

Yale hasn't picked up my key#

Yale hasn't picked up your key yet. It can take up to 10 minutes. Wait a few minutes and press Try again. Yale's key page only loads on campus or on the VPN; use Connect VPN only from the card's ⋮ menu to open it from elsewhere.

"The server looks different from last time"#

This means the cluster's server identity changed since you last connected. It can be a planned change, but check with the cluster's HPC team before you connect.

The cluster says my password is wrong#

For example Wulver didn't accept your UCID or password. Check them in Settings, then try again. Open the gear on the cluster card, then Sign-in, and type the password again. On Expanse, check the code from your authenticator app as well.

The VPN won't start on Windows or macOS#

The first VPN connect needs the VPN helper, which asks for permission once (the Windows prompt, or your Mac password). If you declined it, connect again and approve it.

Globus copies don't start#

  • Globus needs the one-time setup in Files, Transfers.
  • Globus Connect is not running on a cluster: let the app start it on the login node.
  • Globus needs your permission: press Allow.
