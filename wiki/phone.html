Android app

The Android app has the same pages as the desktop, made for a phone. It is not in any store yet.

See Install and update for how to get it.

Sign in by scanning#

  1. On your computer, open Settings in HPC Hub and choose Set up the phone app (or Connect phone in a cluster card's ⋮ menu).
  2. Pick the logins to send. With Include passwords off, the phone asks for each password.
  3. Select Show QR code and scan it with the phone.

The code carries the logins you chose. The phone then connects on its own; Duo asks on the phone where the cluster uses it. For clusters that use SSH keys, the phone gets a key of its own.

Stay connected in the background#

Android can close connections to save battery. In Settings, On the phone, turn on Stay connected in the background and Run without battery limits.

Notifications#

Choose which ones in Settings, Notifications: Duo waiting, a cluster connected, node time almost up, a job ended, a copy finished, scratch files close to deletion, and more.

Terminals on the phone#

  • Swipe to scroll; tap to open the keyboard.
  • The key row has Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Alt and the arrows.
  • The timeline and marks work as on the desktop.

VPN and passwords#

The phone uses the university VPN only for HPC Hub's own connections, and several clusters can be connected at once. Logins are kept in an encrypted file whose key stays in the phone's Keystore.
