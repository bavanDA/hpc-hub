Report a problem

Something broken or confusing? HPC Hub can send a report for you, and you read every word of it before it goes.

Where to find it#

  • Settings, then Report a problem, for anything you noticed.
  • A quiet Report next to an error: on a cluster card that failed to connect, in an error message at the bottom of the window, or on a page that couldn't load. The report then starts with that error.
  • If a page breaks completely, it shows Reload and Report it instead of a blank window. Your clusters, jobs and files are not affected.

Sending a report#

  1. Write what went wrong under What went wrong?: what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  2. If you'd like an answer, add your email. It is optional.
  3. Tick Add a screenshot of the window if a picture helps. It shows whatever is on screen, so look at it first; it appears in the window before anything is sent.
  4. Read Everything that will be sent, then press Send report.

When it has gone, the window says Report sent and gives the report a number. Use that number if you write about it later.

What is sent, and what is not#

  • Nothing is sent until you press Send. Cancel, or close the window, and nothing leaves your computer.
  • You see exactly what is sent. The box Everything that will be sent holds the whole report: the app's version and your system, the page you were on, the error, your note, your email if you gave one, and the app's recent log.
  • Personal details are removed first. Names, folders and addresses are replaced with placeholders such as <user>, <home>, <account> and <ip>, also in what you type.
  • Passwords are never included. Tokens, cookies and keys become <hidden>.

Other ways#

You can also open an issue on the GitHub repository. Issues there are public, so leave out usernames, group names, paths and anything else about you or your work.
