Jobs

The Jobs page shows what is running and waiting on a cluster, in plain words, with details and cancel.

Which jobs#

Pick the cluster at the top of the window, then choose under Which jobs:

  • My jobs: running and waiting now.
  • My group: everyone in your groups. With more than one group, a second list picks one.
  • My history (7 days): your jobs from the last week, finished ones too.
  • All jobs: everyone's jobs on the cluster.

State filters by running, waiting, finished, failed, cancelled or ran out of time. The search box finds a job by id, name, person or queue, for example jdoe.

Reading the table#

ColumnWhat it shows
ID, Name, UserThe Slurm job id, its name and who started it.
StateIn plain words: Running, Finished, Failed, Ran out of time, Ran out of memory and so on.
Time usedHow long it has run, out of the time it asked for.
CPUs · memoryWhat it asked for.
Hours (SU)What it has cost so far.
Node or reasonWhere it runs, or why it is waiting.

The reason a job waits is written out instead of Slurm's code word. Some examples:

  • Waiting: higher-priority jobs go first
  • Waiting: not enough free nodes yet
  • Waiting: maintenance has to finish first
  • Waiting: your group is out of SUs
  • Won't start: longer than this queue allows

On a narrow window some columns are hidden; Details shows everything.

Details and cancel#

Click a job to open its details: the time used, its folders and output files (each with Copy path), and All fields for everything Slurm knows.

The ⋮ on a row has Details and Cancel job. Cancel always asks first. To cancel several, tick them and use the bar that appears.

If the job is the node HPC Hub got for you, cancelling it is Release node, and the app warns that your terminals on that node stop too.

Submitting a job#

Open your Slurm script in Code (from Files, a project or a job's folder). For a Slurm script, the bar above the file has Submit job. The new job shows up here.

Notifications#

While a cluster is connected, HPC Hub checks your jobs about once a minute and tells you when one ends, for example Wulver: job 123456 ended. Clicking the notification opens Jobs.
