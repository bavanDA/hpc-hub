Jobs

Open Jobs, pick a cluster, and select a job to see its state, its output files and why it waits.

See your jobs#

Pick the cluster at the top, then under Which jobs:

  • My jobs: running and waiting now.
  • My group: everyone in your groups.
  • My history (7 days): your jobs from the last week, finished ones too.
  • All jobs: everyone's jobs on the cluster.

State filters by running, waiting, finished and so on. The search box finds a job by id, name, person or queue, for example jdoe.

Select a job for its details: time used, folders and output files (each with Copy path), and All fields for everything Slurm knows.

Submit a job#

Open your Slurm script in Code (from Files, a project or a job's folder). The bar above a Slurm script has Submit job. The new job shows up here.

Why a job waits#

The reason is written out in plain words, for example:

  • Waiting: higher-priority jobs go first
  • Waiting: not enough free nodes yet
  • Waiting: maintenance has to finish first
  • Waiting: your group is out of SUs
  • Won't start: longer than this queue allows

Cancel a job#

The ⋮ on a row has Cancel job, which asks first. To cancel several, tick them and use the bar that appears. Cancelling a job stops everything running in it. For the node HPC Hub got for you, this is Release node, and your terminals on that node stop too.

Notifications#

While a cluster is connected, HPC Hub checks your jobs about once a minute and tells you when one ends. Selecting the notification opens Jobs.
