Usage and SUs

How many SUs your group has left, how fast they are going, who used them, how full your storage is, and how busy the queues are.

What an SU is#

An SU (service unit) is the cluster's currency. One SU is roughly one core for one hour; GPUs, memory and some queues count differently. Your group (your lab's allocation) gets a number of SUs, and every job billed to it uses some. Free queues still count core hours, but cost 0 SU.

Each queue's price is on the Limits tab, and the node window shows the Cost before you ask for a node.

The tabs#

Pick the cluster at the top of the window, then a tab:

Overview#

One card per group you belong to, on every connected cluster. Each card has:

  • The big number: SUs left, or SUs used when the group has no limit.
  • A bar of what is used, with your part in it.
  • A line about the pace, such as About 20 days left at the pace of the last month or Plenty for the year at this pace.
  • SUs per week for the last 12 weeks, where the cluster keeps them.
  • At the bottom, how much you used, and when the allocation ends.

A red tag says Near the limit or Used up. Click a card to see who used it.

Hours#

For one group (Group to bill) and a period (the last 7, 30 or 90 days, or the last year): the hours left or used, and Who used it, one row per person. Click a person to see their jobs on the Jobs page.

Storage#

Your quotas: home, project, scratch and so on, how full each is, and how much of it is yours. Click a row to see who uses a shared space. Scratch rows say when files are deleted, with See what is at risk, which opens Backup.

Queues#

How busy each queue is right now: how many CPUs are busy, how many jobs run and how many wait.

Limits#

The most one job can ask for on each queue, and what it costs. See The Limits tab.

When numbers are missing#

Clusters don't all keep the same records. Where a cluster doesn't say, the app says so instead of guessing, for example SUs per week are not available on this cluster or The pace is not known on this cluster.
