Backup of scratch

Scratch is fast, but most clusters delete old files there. The backup agent finds what is at risk and copies the work that matters to your project folder before it goes.

Why scratch needs a backup#

Each cluster has its own rule for how long files may stay in scratch before they are deleted (purged). When the app was written, the clusters' own docs said:

  • Wulver deletes scratch files after 30 days, and may delete sooner when scratch is very full.
  • Yale deletes scratch files after 30 days. The app reads the number from the cluster when it can.
  • Expanse deletes scratch files 90 days after they were made.

If a cluster states a different number, the app uses the cluster's. HPC Hub never touches files to make them look newer; the clusters don't allow that.

Where to find it#

  • Agents, the Backup tab.
  • Usage, Storage: See what is at risk on a scratch row.
  • A note when scratch files are close to deletion, like Some scratch files go in 5 days (with the date), with See the plan. You also get a notification.

The steps#

The backup goes through five steps, shown at the top: Check, At risk, Plan, Space and Copy.

Check#

Check my scratch looks at every file. It takes about 20 minutes, and you can close the app meanwhile. Let Claude sort unclear folders lets Claude help decide what a folder is; Claude only looks, and can't change or run anything.

At risk#

How many files go in the coming days, how big they are and in which folders, grouped into Could be deleted any day now, Within a week, Within 2 weeks and Later.

Plan#

Each folder is marked Keep or Leave out, and you can change any of them. Options: Copy whole folders so each one stays complete (git repositories are always copied whole), and Only files deleted in the next 14 days.

Space#

How much of your project folder the copy uses, and how full it will be after. If it doesn't fit, Leave out the biggest ones that can be made again or Put code in home. Try it first makes the list of files and checks the space without copying anything.

Copy#

The copy runs on the cluster, so you can close the app. Stop copy keeps what was copied so far; start again later and it goes on from there. At the end, every file is checked against the one in scratch.

Nothing in scratch is changed or deleted. If a file in project is older than the one in scratch, it is replaced and the old one is kept in .hpc-hub-replaced. Files a job is still writing are left for next time, and the app says how many.

Weekly check#

Reminders can run Check scratch every week on a day and time you choose. It runs on the cluster, even with your laptop off. You also choose how early to hear about it, like Tell me when files are 14 days from deletion.
