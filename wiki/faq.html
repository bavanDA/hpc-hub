Questions and answers

Privacy, cost and what keeps running when you close the app.

Where are my passwords kept?#

Usernames and passwords are typed into the app and kept in your system's keyring:

  • Linux: GNOME Keyring (the Secret Service)
  • macOS: the Keychain
  • Windows: Credential Manager
  • Android: an encrypted file whose key stays in the phone's Keystore

Never in plain text, and never in the app's settings files. Globus sign-in happens in your own browser, and its tokens go in the keyring too.

Does HPC Hub send my data anywhere?#

No. There is no tracking and no account with HPC Hub. The app connects to your clusters, their VPN and sign-in pages, and the services you choose to use, such as Globus, or an AI agent's company when you run that agent. The PDF viewer's dictionary and read aloud use online services when you use them.

The one thing HPC Hub ever sends about you or the app is a problem report, only when you press Send, and only after you have read it.

Does HPC Hub cost SUs?#

The app itself doesn't. Your compute node, VS Code and agents are Slurm jobs, and the cluster bills them like any other job, by the queue and priority you pick. The node window shows the cost before you ask for a node. A whole-node queue bills for the whole node; see what a whole-node queue means.

What keeps running when I close the app?#

Terminals, agents, VS Code and your compute node keep running on the cluster, until you stop them or the node's time is up. Globus transfers, backup copies and scheduled agents run on their own too. Copies through this laptop need the app open.

To release your nodes as you leave, use Settings, Disconnect all and quit. Jobs you started yourself and VS Code jobs keep running.

Can I use a cluster that isn't listed?#

HPC Hub comes set up for Wulver (NJIT), Bouchet (Yale) and Expanse (SDSC). Show or hide them in Settings, Clusters.

Can I use the phone and the computer at the same time?#

Yes. Both connect to the same clusters, see the same sessions and the same terminal marks. Duo asks on each device when it connects.

Why does my system warn me when I open the app?#

The installers are not signed yet. See Install for how to open it the first time.

Is it open source?#

Yes, under the MIT license. The code is on GitHub.

How do I change the colors?#

Settings, Appearance. System follows your computer's light or dark mode, and there are a few more themes to pick from.
